# Drill 1: True / False / Not Given (TFNG) Precision Test

> **Цель:** Проверить калибровку на логику составителей экзамена IELTS (Cambridge) и исключить домысливание контекста.  
> **Инструкция:** Прочитай 3 коротких академических фрагмента ниже и определи для каждого утверждения:
> - **TRUE** — если утверждение напрямую согласуется с информацией в тексте.
> - **FALSE** — если утверждение прямо противоречит информации в тексте (текст говорит обратное).
> - **NOT GIVEN** — если в тексте нет достаточной информации, чтобы подтвердить или опровергнуть утверждение.

### Passage 1: Autonomous Systems & Urban Transport

> "The introduction of autonomous electric shuttles in semi-pedestrianised zones has shown a measurable reduction in minor traffic collisions over a six-month trial. Urban planners noted that although initial public scepticism was pronounced, passenger satisfaction ratings escalated steadily once the vehicles demonstrated consistent adherence to speed limits. Nevertheless, local municipal authorities have decided against expanding the fleet to peripheral suburban routes until a unified emergency-braking standard is formally ratified by the national transport board."

**Statements for Passage 1:**
1. *Commuters were enthusiastic about the autonomous shuttle service when it was first launched.*
2. *The national transport board has already established comprehensive safety regulations for autonomous vehicle braking systems.*
3. *Suburban residents lodged formal complaints regarding the exclusion of their neighbourhoods from the pilot project.*
